What's new in 1.3.0

- The dealer talks you through its turn: what it turns over, its running total, and "Dealer busts" when it goes over.
- Your own hand is called too - your total when the turn reaches you, and a bust or a push as it happens.
- A redesigned table: clearer felt, readable colours at every text size, and layouts that fit landscape and tablets.
- The back button returns to the lobby instead of quitting the game.
