What's new in 1.2.0

- A real human voice: every call-out re-recorded, and the table no longer talks over itself.
- Your hand total now sits in a circle beside your bet, and is spoken as each card lands.
- Voice can be turned off, in the opening setup or in Settings.
- The table only announces a sweep pot when there is one to win.
- Google Sign-In fixed, and a slow network can no longer hang startup.
- Long names and large text no longer push buttons off screen.
