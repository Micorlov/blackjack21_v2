Colors

A dark-leather room around a green felt table, with brass/gold as the single accent. Text that sits on the felt or on a panel has its own lighter tokens so it keeps 4.5:1.

Typography

Four faces: Sora for UI and card ranks, Instrument Serif Italic for titles, Space Mono for every number, Cinzel only for the print on the felt (Latin languages). Nothing renders below 12.
