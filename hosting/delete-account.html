Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 8, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Blackjack 21 online account and the records the game keeps about you. There is no form that deletes anything automatically on this page: a request is an email that we read and act on ourselves, and we will delete the records we can match to it within 30 days.

Fastest: delete it in the app

In version 1.8.4 and later: Settings → Privacy → Delete account and data. This deletes your own online records and account straight away, and it also removes your ID from the group and invite pointers that name you. See the privacy policy for exactly what it removes.

Or ask us by email

Use this if you cannot open the app, or have uninstalled it.

Email a deletion request to micorlov@gmail.com

Please include, in this order of preference:

  • your Account ID: open the app, then Settings → Privacy → Account ID and tap it to copy (it is a random identifier for your online account, so share it only for this request);
  • if you cannot open the app, the name that shows for you on the leaderboard (a guest shows as “Guest” followed by four letters or digits; a Google user shows their Google display name) and your 6-character friends-group code, if you joined or created one;
  • if you signed in with Google, the email address of that Google account, so we can find the sign-in record.

Do not send passwords or payment details: the game has none of them. We can only delete records we can match to what you send. If we cannot find a record we will reply and say so.

What is deleted, and what is not

  • Deleted: your leaderboard record, your player record, your member row in your friends group, and your Firebase Authentication account (including the Google email held there). Your ID is also removed from the group record of a group you created and from other members' invite pointers (replaced by “deleted”).
  • Not deleted: your save file on your phone (uninstalling the app removes it); the record of a friends group you created, which keeps its code and creation time (with your ID replaced by “deleted”), and other members' records; install and error records, which contain no name or account ID and so cannot be matched to you; and data Google holds for Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob, which is handled under Google's policies.
